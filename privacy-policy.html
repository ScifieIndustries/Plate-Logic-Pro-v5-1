Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Plate Logic respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information Plate Logic collects, how it is used, where it is stored, and how you can request deletion of your account and associated data.

1. Information We Collect

When you create and use a Plate Logic account, we may process:

  • Email address — used for account login and authentication.
  • User ID — an internal identifier associated with your account.
  • Account creation date and time — associated with your account in the authentication system.
  • Authentication information — information necessary to provide secure account access, including login and session information.
  • Subscription information — if you use a paid plan, including your selected plan, subscription status, Stripe customer ID, Stripe subscription ID, and related account timestamps.

Plate Logic does not require your name, telephone number, postal address, or GPS/location data for account use.

Plate Logic does not use photographs or AI systems to process your personal data.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use account information to:

  • create and maintain your Plate Logic account;
  • authenticate you when you log in;
  • provide free and paid Plate Logic features;
  • manage subscription status and access;
  • process account deletion requests;
  • maintain security and prevent abuse; and
  • communicate with you when necessary regarding your account or support requests.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Authentication and Database

Plate Logic uses Supabase for authentication and database services.

Your account information and subscription-related account data may therefore be processed and stored through Supabase's services.

Account deletion is performed server-side. When you delete your Plate Logic account through the account deletion feature, Plate Logic deletes the associated Supabase authentication account and Plate Logic subscription/entitlement data.

4. Payments and Subscriptions

Plate Logic uses Stripe to process paid subscriptions and payments.

Stripe may process payment, billing, transaction, customer, and technical information necessary to provide its payment and subscription services.

Plate Logic does not store your full payment card number or CVC.

Plate Logic stores certain Stripe identifiers associated with your account, such as your Stripe customer ID and subscription ID, in order to manage your subscription.

When you delete your Plate Logic account while an active Stripe subscription exists, Plate Logic attempts to cancel the active subscription before deleting the account.

Stripe may retain certain information where required or permitted for legitimate purposes such as legal, regulatory, security, fraud-prevention, accounting, or transaction-record requirements. Such retention is governed by Stripe's own policies and legal obligations.

5. Analytics and Tracking

Plate Logic does not currently use Google Analytics or Firebase Analytics.

Plate Logic does not use GPS or location tracking.

Plate Logic may contain links to external websites or services. For example, Plate Logic may link to Smol Launch. External websites operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

6. Account Deletion

You can delete your Plate Logic account directly from within Plate Logic using the Delete Account option.

When you confirm account deletion, Plate Logic:

  1. verifies your authenticated account;
  2. checks whether a Stripe subscription is associated with your account;
  3. cancels an active Stripe subscription where applicable;
  4. deletes your Plate Logic subscription/entitlement data; and
  5. deletes your Plate Logic authentication account.

Account deletion is intended to permanently remove the account data held by Plate Logic.

You can also request account deletion without using the app through our web-based account deletion resource:

https://platelogic.dk/delete-account.html

If you cannot use the in-app deletion feature, you can use the web resource to submit an account deletion request.

7. Data Retention

When an account is deleted, Plate Logic deletes the associated account data that it controls as part of the account deletion process.

Certain information may nevertheless be retained where necessary for legitimate purposes, including:

  • legal or regulatory obligations;
  • accounting or financial recordkeeping;
  • security and fraud prevention;
  • resolving disputes; or
  • enforcing legal rights.

Third-party service providers, including Stripe and Supabase, may also retain information according to their own legal obligations and applicable privacy policies.

8. Data Security

Plate Logic uses appropriate technical and organizational measures intended to protect account information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Sensitive server-side credentials are not intended to be exposed through the Plate Logic website or client-side application.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's Privacy

Plate Logic is not specifically directed at children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children where such collection is prohibited by applicable law.

10. Your Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including rights to:

  • request access to personal information;
  • request correction of inaccurate information;
  • request deletion of your account and personal information;
  • object to or restrict certain processing; and
  • contact a relevant data protection authority.
