Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Plate Logic respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information Plate Logic collects, how it is used, where it is processed, and how you can request deletion of your account and associated personal data.

1. Information We Collect

When you create and use a Plate Logic account, we may collect and process:

  • Email address — used to create and identify your account, provide login and authentication, and support account recovery.
  • User ID — an internal identifier associated with your account.
  • Account information — including account creation date and time, authentication information, and account status.
  • Subscription information — if you use a paid plan, including your selected plan, subscription status, Stripe customer ID, Stripe subscription ID, and relevant subscription timestamps.

Plate Logic does not require your name, telephone number, postal address, or GPS/location data for account use.

Plate Logic does not require users to upload photos and does not use AI to analyze user photos or personal information.

2. Account Creation and Login

Users create an account using their email address and verify their email address through a one-time login code or link.

Plate Logic does not require users to create a password when registering an account.

For existing accounts, password-based login may be enabled upon request or through the account recovery process. A password is therefore optional and is not required to create an account.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use account information to:

  • provide and operate Plate Logic;
  • create and maintain user accounts;
  • authenticate users;
  • provide account recovery;
  • provide free and paid features;
  • manage subscriptions and access to paid features;
  • process and manage payments through our payment provider;
  • process account deletion requests;
  • maintain security and prevent abuse; and
  • respond to support requests.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Service Providers

Supabase

Plate Logic uses Supabase for authentication, database services, and related application infrastructure. Information processed by Supabase may include your email address, internal user ID, authentication information, and subscription-related account information.

Stripe

Plate Logic uses Stripe to process payments and manage subscriptions. Stripe may process information necessary for payment and billing, such as payment, transaction, customer, and subscription information.

Plate Logic stores relevant Stripe identifiers, such as the Stripe customer ID and subscription ID, to manage your subscription. Plate Logic does not store your full payment card number or CVC/security code.

5. Analytics and Tracking

Plate Logic does not currently use Google Analytics or Firebase Analytics.

Plate Logic does not use GPS or precise location data.

Plate Logic does not require users to upload photos.

Plate Logic may contain links to external websites or services. External websites operate under their own privacy policies and data practices.

6. Data Sharing

Plate Logic does not sell your personal information.

Personal information may be processed by service providers such as Supabase and Stripe where necessary to provide authentication, database, payment, subscription, and related services.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or when necessary to protect the security, rights, or property of Plate Logic, its users, or others.

7. Data Retention

We retain account and subscription information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain account records, handle subscriptions, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

Some information may need to be retained for a limited period after account deletion where required for legal, accounting, security, fraud-prevention, or other legitimate purposes.

Third-party providers such as Stripe and Supabase may also retain information according to their own policies and legal obligations.

8. Account Deletion

You can delete your Plate Logic account directly from within Plate Logic using the account deletion feature.

When account deletion is processed, Plate Logic verifies your authenticated account, cancels an active Stripe subscription where applicable, deletes the associated Plate Logic subscription/entitlement data, and deletes the Plate Logic authentication account.

Account deletion is intended to permanently remove the account data held by Plate Logic, subject to applicable retention requirements.

You can also request account deletion without using the app through our web-based account deletion resource:

https://platelogic.dk/delete-account.html

9. Data Security

Plate Logic takes reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect account and personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

10. Children's Privacy

Plate Logic is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to Plate Logic without appropriate authorization, please contact us so that we can review and take appropriate action.

11. Your Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to:

  • request access to your personal information;
  • request correction of inaccurate information;
  • request deletion of your personal information;
  • request restriction of certain processing;
  • object to certain processing; and
  • request data portability where applicable.

To exercise your rights or ask questions about your personal information, contact us using the email address below.

12. International Data Processing

Plate Logic uses third-party service providers that may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Where required by applicable law, appropriate safeguards are used for international transfers of personal information.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Plate Logic, our services, or applicable legal requirements.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
